3. How your order travels

This website does not store your order on any server. When you press “Send order by WhatsApp”, WhatsApp opens on your device with the order already written, and you send it. From then on, WhatsApp (Meta) handles the message under its own privacy policy, and the restaurant receives it on its WhatsApp number.

4. How long we keep it

Order data is kept only as long as needed to prepare and deliver the order and, afterwards, for the period in which legal liabilities may arise. Data in server logs is kept for the short period set by the hosting provider.

5. Who receives your data

  • Netlify, Inc., website hosting (United States). It may process visitors’ IP addresses in server logs, with the safeguards the GDPR requires for international transfers.
  • Meta (WhatsApp), when you send an order.
  • Google, if you view the embedded map (see the cookie policy).

We do not sell your data or use it for marketing.
